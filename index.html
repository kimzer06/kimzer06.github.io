Project SoulSmith

Personal project · Hades-inspired action combat
Solo, in development2026Unity 6 · URP · Odin · DOTween · MM Feedbacks
  • Combat. A Hitbox → Hurtbox → Receiver damage pipeline with allocation-free hit payloads, a decaying stagger gauge, and attack speed that scales windup, active and recovery frames.
  • Inventory and equipment. Item data in ScriptableObjects, runtime items as serializable C# instances so stacks, rarity rolls and IDs survive saving. Weapon, four rune slots and armor sit behind a provider interface, with drag-and-drop UI and stat allocation.
  • Data-driven. Attacks, weapons and runes are authored as assets; an AnimatorOverrideController swaps clips per weapon and rune at runtime.
  • Structure. One assembly per system, non-alloc queries on hot paths, and a shared stat-modifier pipeline for gear, buffs and progression.

Computer Assembly Shop Simulator

University project · VR PC-building shop
Solo, 3rd year2025Unity · C# · XR Interaction Toolkit
  • Shop loop. Buy parts from priced shelves, unbox them, and build; a packaging zone adds up part prices as items drop in.
  • VR assembly. A snap system checks each part by tag, locks it into the right motherboard socket (GPU, CPU, cooler, RAM) and switches physics off so it reads as seated.
  • Customers. NavMesh NPCs walk in and request one of several preset builds, shown on in-world signs with audio cues.

About

I studied Interactive Design & Game at Sripatum University. Most of what I know comes from one long project with a game designer and four artists, where I was the only programmer.

That meant owning everything that runs: the controller, the netcode, the UI, the cutscene plumbing, and the tools that let the rest of the team build levels without waiting on me. I learned networking on Netcode for GameObjects, then moved the finished game to Photon Fusion 2, so I think in terms of authority and sync rather than one framework's API.

I like code that is boring to read and systems that stay in sync, and I check things in real builds on two PCs before calling them done.
